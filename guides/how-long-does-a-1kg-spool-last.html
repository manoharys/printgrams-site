How long does a 1 kg spool of filament last?

Updated 10 November 2026

A 1 kg spool of 1.75 mm PLA holds about 335 m of filament. That’s enough for roughly 80 Benchies, or 10–20 medium prints of 50–100 g each. Most hobbyists who print a few times a week get through a spool in 2–6 weeks.

Metres in a 1 kg spool, by material

One metre of 1.75 mm filament is 2.405 cm³, so the length depends on the density:

Material Density Metres per kg (1.75 mm) Metres per kg (2.85 mm)
PLA 1.24 g/cm³ ~335 m ~126 m
PETG 1.27 g/cm³ ~327 m ~123 m
ABS 1.04 g/cm³ ~400 m ~151 m
ASA 1.07 g/cm³ ~389 m ~147 m
TPU 1.21 g/cm³ ~344 m ~129 m
Nylon (PA) 1.14 g/cm³ ~365 m ~138 m

Use the length and weight converter for other materials and diameters.

How many prints that is

Print Typical weight Per 1 kg spool
3DBenchy ~12 g ~80
Phone stand ~30 g ~33
Planter or vase (vase mode) ~60 g ~16
Helmet or large cosplay part ~400–800 g 1–2

Each print also wastes a little filament on the purge line, skirt and supports. Multi-colour printers with an AMS or MMU can waste much more, often 20–50% on prints with many colour changes. Our guide to reducing AMS purge waste covers how to cut that down.

Finding out how much is left

Weigh the spool on a kitchen scale and subtract the weight of the empty spool. Empty spools weigh 120–260 g depending on the brand, and the spool remaining calculator includes those weights for 33 brands. It also tells you whether there’s enough left for your next print.
