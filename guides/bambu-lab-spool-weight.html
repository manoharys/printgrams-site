Bambu Lab spool weight: empty, full and refill

Updated 14 November 2026

An empty Bambu Lab spool weighs about 255 g as shipped: about 213 g for the reusable plastic spool plus about 40 g for the cardboard core the filament is wound on. A full 1 kg spool therefore reads about 1,255 g on a kitchen scale.

The numbers

What you are weighing Typical weight Range seen
Reusable plastic spool, bare (no core) 213 g 208–216 g
Spool + cardboard core, as shipped 255 g 250–260 g
Refill cardboard core only 40 g 37–44 g
Full 1 kg spool, as shipped ~1,255 g —

The plastic spool separates into two halves and clips around a refill. That is why the empty weight depends on whether the cardboard core is still inside.

How much filament is left?

Weigh the spool, then subtract:

  • 255 g if it is a factory spool with the core still in it;
  • 213 g if you removed the core, or are reusing the spool with your own winding;
  • 40 g if you are weighing a refill on its own, outside the plastic spool.

For example, a factory spool that reads 610 g has about 610 − 255 = 355 g of filament left. For PLA at 1.75 mm that is roughly 119 m, enough for most prints under 300 g with a margin for purges. The spool remaining calculator does the subtraction for Bambu and 30+ other brands and converts the result to metres; the grams to metres converter works for any material.

Will the AMS tell me?

The AMS can show an estimate of remaining filament for Bambu spools with RFID tags, but it is an estimate based on tracked usage, not a measurement, and it does not work for third-party spools. A scale is the reliable check, especially for spools that have been used on other machines or rewound.

Other brands

Empty-spool weights vary from about 125 g (thin cardboard) to 300 g (heavy plastic). Prusament is about 194 g, eSUN about 230 g on plastic and 160 g on cardboard, Polymaker cardboard about 140 g. The full list with measured ranges is in the empty spool weights table.

Save the plastic spool

Bambu sells filament as refills on a bare cardboard core, slightly cheaper than with a spool. Keep one or two empty plastic spools and you can buy refills from then on. If you reduce waste elsewhere too, cutting AMS purge waste saves more filament than most people expect.
