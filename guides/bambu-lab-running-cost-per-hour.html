What does a Bambu Lab printer cost to run per hour?

Updated 12 November 2026

Printing PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 mini, A1 or P1S costs about $0.20–0.60 per hour in total. Filament is most of it, and electricity is only 1–3 US cents per hour.

Electricity per hour

These are typical measured averages while printing PLA. Peaks are higher, but the bed and hotend cycle on and off once they’re up to temperature.

Printer Avg. power (PLA) US ($0.17/kWh) UK (£0.26/kWh) Germany (€0.38/kWh)
A1 mini ~80 W $0.014/h £0.021/h €0.030/h
A1 ~95 W $0.016/h £0.025/h €0.036/h
P1S ~105 W $0.018/h £0.027/h €0.040/h
X1 Carbon (ABS, enclosed) ~150 W $0.026/h £0.039/h €0.057/h

Use the electricity cost calculator with your own tariff.

Filament per hour

At normal speeds with a 0.4 mm nozzle, these printers use roughly 10–25 g of filament per hour on typical models. Large prints with sparse infill and fast settings use more. At $20/kg that’s $0.20–0.50 per hour.

Wear and parts

Nozzles, build plates, PTFE tubes and belts wear out over time. A fair allowance is $0.02–0.10 per hour, depending on how hard you run the printer and whether you print abrasive filaments.

Your exact figure
