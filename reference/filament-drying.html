Filament drying temperatures and times

Keep the drying temperature below the material's glass transition or the spool fuses. These are the figures manufacturers publish, sorted hottest first.

Quick answers

  • PLA: 45–50 °C for 4–6 hours. Never above 55 °C.
  • PETG: 65 °C for about 6 hours. At 45–50 °C allow 12 hours or more.
  • PCTG: 65 °C for about 6 hours.
  • ABS and ASA: 70 °C for about 6 hours.
  • TPU: 50–70 °C for 6–8 hours. Check the maker's datasheet, because grades differ.
  • Nylon (PA): 80 °C for 12 hours, then print straight from a dry box.

Rules that hold for every filament

  • Dry below the glass-transition temperature: PLA ≤ 50 °C, PETG ≤ 65 °C, ABS/ASA ≤ 70–80 °C, nylon and PC ≤ 80–90 °C. Filament dryers with a fan reach the core of the spool faster than still air.
  • Time matters more than temperature. A full 1 kg spool needs 6–12 hours; the outer layers dry in two.
  • Weigh before and after. A spool that loses 5–10 g has given up a lot of water; when the weight stops dropping it is dry.
  • Storage is the other half: a sealed box with silica gel at 10–20% relative humidity keeps a dried spool dry for months.

Questions people ask

How do I know my filament is wet?

Popping or hissing at the nozzle, steam, stringing that was not there before, rough or matte surfaces on glossy filament, and weak layer bonding. PETG, nylon, TPU and PVA show it first.

Can I dry filament in a kitchen oven?

Only if the oven holds a low temperature accurately; many overshoot by 10–20 °C and will soften PLA (glass transition ~60 °C). A filament dryer, a food dehydrator, or a dry box with desiccant is safer.

How long does dried filament stay dry?

In open air at 50% humidity PETG and nylon reabsorb enough moisture to matter within 1–3 days. Store opened spools in a sealed box or bag with desiccant, or print from a dry box.

How long does it take to dry PETG?

About 6 hours at 65 °C for a full 1 kg spool in a filament dryer, or 4 hours for a part-used spool. Below 55 °C it takes much longer: 8–12 hours or more.

Will PETG dry at 45 °C?

Slowly. 45 °C removes some moisture from the outer layers, but a wet PETG spool needs about 12–24 hours at that temperature. 60–65 °C dries it in about 6 hours. Stay below about 70 °C so the spool does not soften.

What temperature and time should I use for PCTG?

Dry PCTG at 65 °C for about 6 hours, the same as PETG. It is less hygroscopic than nylon but picks up moisture within days in humid air.

What is the drying temperature for ABS and ASA?

70 °C for about 6 hours. ABS absorbs less moisture than PETG, so spools that have been stored sealed often print fine without drying.

Does PLA need drying?

Rarely for fresh spools, but PLA that has been open for months in a humid room prints noticeably better after 4–6 hours at 45–50 °C.
