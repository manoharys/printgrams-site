Questions people ask

How much does an empty Bambu Lab spool weigh?

About 213 g for the bare reusable plastic spool, or 255 g as shipped with the cardboard core still inside. A full factory 1 kg Bambu spool therefore reads roughly 1255 g on the scale. The refill cardboard core alone is about 40 g.

How do I work out how much filament is left?

Weigh the whole spool, subtract the empty-spool weight from this table, and you have the grams of filament. The spool remaining calculator turns that into metres and tells you whether your next print will finish.

Why do weights vary between spools of the same brand?

Moulded plastic spools vary by ±5 g, cardboard by ±10 g with humidity, and brands change suppliers. If you need precision, weigh your own empty spool once and write it on the flange.
