Questions about the Formlabs Form 4

How many watts does the Formlabs Form 4 use?

About 100 W on average while printing PLA, 15 W when idle and up to 480 W while heating. Formlabs tech specs: 100-240 VAC, 4.8 A, 480 W; printing average estimated (heated resin tank and LCD array cycle below the rating).

How much does it cost to run a Formlabs Form 4 for 24 hours?

2.4 kWh, which is $0.41 at $0.17/kWh (US average) or about £0.62 at UK rates.

Does the Formlabs Form 4 use a lot of electricity?

No. Running it 8 hours a day for a month costs roughly $4.08 at US rates — comparable to a desktop computer.
