Layer heights that suit the Original Prusa MINI+'s Z axis

Z moves 0.0200 mm per full step (lead screw, 4 mm per motor revolution, 200 steps/rev, 400 steps/mm). Whole multiples of that are "magic" heights: every layer lands on a full step instead of a less accurate microstep.

Questions about the Original Prusa MINI+

How many watts does the Original Prusa MINI+ use?

About 65 W on average while printing PLA, 8 W when idle and up to 150 W while heating. Prusa forum owner wattmeter readings: 40-90 W while printing PLA, ~148 W peak while heating; 160 W Mean Well PSU.

How much does it cost to run a Original Prusa MINI+ for 24 hours?

1.6 kWh, which is $0.27 at $0.17/kWh (US average) or about £0.41 at UK rates.

Does the Original Prusa MINI+ use a lot of electricity?

No. Running it 8 hours a day for a month costs roughly $2.65 at US rates — comparable to a desktop computer.

What are the best layer heights for the Original Prusa MINI+?

The Original Prusa MINI+'s Z axis moves 0.0200 mm per full motor step (4 mm per revolution, 200 steps). Layer heights that are whole multiples of that — 0.08, 0.1, 0.12, 0.14, 0.16, 0.18, 0.2 mm — avoid microstep rounding. 0.15, 0.25 mm are not exact multiples.
