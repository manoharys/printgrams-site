Common questions

How do I calculate the cost of a 3D print?

Multiply the grams from your slicer by your spool price per gram (a $20 kg spool is 2¢ per gram), then add electricity (watts × hours ÷ 1000 × your rate), printer wear and a failure allowance. The print cost calculator does all of it from grams and hours.

How many metres are in a 1 kg spool of filament?

About 335 m of 1.75 mm PLA, 327 m of PETG and 400 m of ABS. It depends on density; the grams to metres converter works for any material and diameter.

How do I know how much filament is left on a spool?

Weigh the spool and subtract the empty spool weight for your brand (for example about 213 g for a bare Bambu Lab spool or 194 g for Prusament). The spool remaining calculator lists 30+ brands and converts the result to metres.

Do I need to upload my STL file?

No. The STL analyzer reads the file inside your browser to measure size, volume, weight and print time. Nothing is uploaded.

Why trust these numbers

Every calculator shows its formula and the data it used. Densities come from manufacturer datasheets, printer wattages from plug-meter measurements where they exist (and are labelled “estimated” where they do not), and marketplace fees from the platforms' published 2026 schedules. If you find a number that is wrong, tell us and we will fix it with a source.
