ABS-like resin resin: the numbers

Impact-modified photopolymer resin. Typical uses: functional prototypes, snap-fit housings, tabletop terrain, drillable and tappable parts.

1.12 g/cm³density (1.05–1.2)
1%linear shrinkage
$30typical per litre ($22–45)
65 °Cmax service temperature
2/5difficulty · medium strength · rigid

Printing tips

  • Slightly longer exposure than standard resin; calibrate with a test print.
  • Do not over-cure; excess UV makes it brittle again.
  • Good general-purpose choice if you print both models and parts.

At ~$30 per litre, a 30 mL miniature uses about $0.90 of resin before IPA, electricity and failures. Use the resin cost calculator for your figures.
