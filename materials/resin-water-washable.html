Water-washable resin in practice

Water-washable resin rinses clean in water instead of IPA, which makes it popular for home printing, classrooms and anyone who would rather not keep litres of alcohol around. Miniatures and hobby models are its usual uses, at about $28 per kilogram.

Washing in water does not make the waste harmless. Never pour rinse water down the drain: leave it in sunlight until the resin in it has cured, filter out the solids and dispose of them as solid waste. Let parts dry completely before UV curing, or the surface stays tacky and can crack.

It is more brittle than standard resin and absorbs moisture over time, so keep it for display pieces rather than load-bearing parts. The resin cost calculator works out the cost of a print from your slicer volume.

Printing tips

  • Wash in water, but never pour rinse water down the drain; cure it in sunlight and dispose as solid waste.
  • Dry fully before UV curing or the surface stays tacky and can crack.
  • More brittle and absorbs moisture over time; not for load-bearing parts.
