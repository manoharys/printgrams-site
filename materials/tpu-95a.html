Printing tips

  • Direct-drive extruder strongly preferred; slow to 20-40 mm/s on Bowden.
  • Minimal or zero retraction to avoid grinding the filament.
  • Dry before printing; wet TPU strings and bubbles badly.

Questions about TPU 95A

What temperature should I print TPU 95A at?

Nozzle 220–240 °C and bed 30–50 °C are the usual range. Start in the middle and tune with a temperature tower; no enclosure is needed.

How many metres of TPU 95A are in a 1 kg spool?

About 344 m at 1.75 mm (density 1.21 g/cm³). One metre weighs 2.91 g.

Does TPU 95A need drying?

Yes — it is moderately hygroscopic. Dry at 70 °C for 6 hours before printing if it has been open for more than a few days.

How much does TPU 95A shrink?

About 0.8% linear. To compensate, scale X and Y by 100.81% in the slicer, or measure a test print and use the shrinkage calculator.
