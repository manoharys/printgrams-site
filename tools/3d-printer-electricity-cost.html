How it's calculated

kWh  = average watts × hours / 1000
cost = kWh × price per kWh
  • Average draw while printing matters, not the power supply rating. A 350 W supply on an Ender 3 typically averages 100–120 W printing PLA because the bed cycles on and off.
  • Enclosed printers with heated chambers and high bed temperatures (ABS, ASA, PC) draw noticeably more — often 150–250 W averaged.
  • Tariffs are national residential averages for 2026; replace with the figure from your bill for an exact answer.

Questions people ask

How much electricity does a 3D printer use per hour?

Most desktop FDM printers average 50–150 W while printing PLA, which is 0.05–0.15 kWh per hour — about $0.01–$0.03 per hour at US rates.

How much does it cost to run a 3D printer 24/7?

At 100 W average and $0.17/kWh: 2.4 kWh per day, about $0.41 per day or $12 per month.

Does a 3D printer use a lot of electricity?

No. A 10-hour print on a typical printer uses about 1 kWh — less than a single load in a tumble dryer.
