How it's calculated

price = (profit + fixed fees + unit cost + actual shipping − shipping charged × (1 − fee %)) / (1 − fee %)
fees apply to item price + shipping charged (as Etsy and eBay do)
  • Marketplaces charge a percentage of the whole order including shipping, plus small fixed fees. Charging “cost × 2” ignores this and quietly eats your margin.
  • We solve the equation backwards: the price that, after every fee, leaves exactly the profit you asked for.
  • Fee schedules are the 2026 published US rates for Etsy, eBay, Amazon Handmade, Shopify Payments and Square. Edit them if your country differs.

Questions people ask

How much should I charge for 3D prints?

A common floor is 2–3× your true cost (material + electricity + wear + labour), then adjust to the market. For a $2 part that means $5–7 before fees; after Etsy fees you need roughly $6–8 to keep the same profit.

What are Etsy fees in 2026?

6.5% transaction fee on the item plus shipping, 3% + $0.25 payment processing in the US, and a $0.20 listing fee per sale. Offsite Ads take another 12–15% when a sale comes from one.

Can I still sell 3D prints on Etsy?

Yes. Etsy's Creativity Standards (July 2024, clarified for 3D prints in 2025) require items to be made or designed by you: your own designs or designs made to your specification qualify, a purchased STL printed as-is generally does not. Read the current standards before listing.
