How it's calculated

resin       = mL / 1000 × price per litre
IPA         = mL used / 1000 × price per litre
electricity = watts × hours / 1000 × rate
true cost   = (resin + IPA + electricity + wear + labour + consumables) / (1 − failure rate)
  • Resin printers draw 30–80 W, so electricity is small; resin and consumables dominate.
  • Washing alcohol is reused but still consumed; 10–30 mL per small print is typical once you account for top-ups.
  • Failure rates on resin are higher than FDM for new users (10–20%), which is why the allowance matters.

Questions people ask

How much does resin 3D printing cost per print?

A 30 mL miniature at $30/L resin costs about $0.90 in resin, plus roughly $0.20 in IPA and consumables, plus a cent or two of electricity.

How many prints does a litre of resin make?

Divide 1,000 by the mL per print from your slicer. At 30 mL per miniature that is about 33 prints per litre before failures.
